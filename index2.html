01 / About

Clear advice.
Careful work.

I am a licensed paralegal and notary public serving clients across Ontario.

I assist individuals and small businesses with matters before tribunals and courts, within the authorized scope of practice for paralegals. My approach is straightforward: clear communication, careful preparation, and respectful service at every stage.

02 / Practice areas

Where I
can help.

Licensed paralegal and notary public support across Ontario, with clear guidance for the legal issues in front of you.

01

Traffic & provincial offences

Speeding, stunt driving, careless driving, insurance, distracted driving, red light and stop sign violations, parking tickets, and municipal by-law matters. Services include Provincial Offences Court appearances, prosecutor negotiations, and trial representation.

02

Landlord & tenant disputes

Representation before the Landlord and Tenant Board for non-payment and personal-use evictions, tenant rights, rent arrears and increases, maintenance issues, lease disputes, and termination matters.

03

Small claims court

Support for claims up to $50,000, including debt recovery, breach of contract, property damage, and negligence claims, including claims, defences, documents, settlement negotiations, motions, and trial representation.

04

Immigration & refugee board

Representation before the Immigration and Refugee Board, including RPD refugee protection hearings, Basis of Claim (BOC) forms, evidence review, legal submissions, and hearing preparation.

05

Notary & commissioner services

Commissioning affidavits and statutory declarations, certifying true copies, and supporting sworn legal, immigration, and business documents by appointment, in person or virtually.

06

Referral services

When a matter falls outside my scope, I can help connect you with an appropriate legal professional.
