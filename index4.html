I am a licensed paralegal and notary public serving clients across Ontario. I help make unfamiliar legal processes easier to understand and easier to act on.

Every matter begins with listening carefully, identifying what is in front of you, and building a practical path forward within the authorized scope of practice for paralegals.

Practice areas

Where I can help.

Focused representation and document support for the legal issues in front of you.

01

Traffic & provincial offences

Support for traffic tickets, driving charges, insurance matters, municipal by-laws, negotiations, and Provincial Offences Court appearances.

02

Landlord & tenant disputes

Representation before the Landlord and Tenant Board for arrears, evictions, maintenance issues, lease disputes, and termination matters.

03

Small claims court

Help with claims up to $50,000, including contracts, debt recovery, property damage, defences, motions, and settlement.

04

Immigration & refugee board

Support with RPD hearings, Basis of Claim forms, evidence review, legal submissions, and hearing preparation.

05

Notary & commissioner

Commissioning affidavits, certifying true copies, and supporting sworn legal, immigration, and business documents.

06

Referral services

When a matter falls outside my scope, I can help connect you with an appropriate legal professional.
